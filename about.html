Our Story

Furniture Built to Live In, Not Just Look At

What started as a small woodworking studio in Portland has grown into a furniture brand trusted by thousands of homes — but our approach hasn't changed. Every piece is designed to be functional first, beautiful always, and built to last well beyond trends.

We work directly with hardwood mills and textile weavers to keep quality high and prices honest, cutting out the markup that comes from middlemen.

Mission & Vision

What Drives Us

Mission: To make thoughtfully designed, sustainably built furniture accessible to every home, without compromising on craft.

Vision: A future where furniture is bought once, loved for decades, and passed down rather than replaced.

Timeline

Our Journey

2008

Founded in a Portland Workshop

Furnish & Co. began as a two-person woodworking studio building made-to-order tables for local homes.

2013

First Flagship Showroom

Opened our first showroom, expanding into upholstered seating and case goods.

2018

Sustainable Sourcing Pledge

Committed to 100% FSC-certified hardwood and low-VOC finishes across the entire catalog.

2022

Nationwide Delivery Network

Launched white-glove delivery in all 50 states with in-house logistics.

2026

12,000+ Homes Furnished

Today our pieces sit in living rooms, offices and gardens across the country.
